The cat app that used 12% of the CPU doing nothing

Being fond of cats, I wanted one on my Mac. So I built one, without writing a line of code.

It is called Cataholic. A chunky cat that sits on top of your windows, waves when you come back, gets the zoomies and naps. Double-click her and she hides in a little kingdom for you to find. Eight coats, and up to a hundred cats if one is not enough.

An early version had a problem. The cat was sitting still, and the app was using 12% of the CPU. Doing nothing.

"No theatrics," I said. Now, sitting still, she is one still picture. Almost no CPU.

If something is idle, it should be idle.

That rule goes well beyond a cat app. A process that looks busy while producing nothing still costs something, and nothing visibly breaks to tell you.

Cataholic is free and open source, notarized by Apple, and now on Windows too. Cataholic on GitHub ↗
