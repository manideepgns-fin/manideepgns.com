## LinkedIn

My cat app once used 12% of the CPU while doing nothing.

Cataholic is a cat that lives on your Mac. It sits still while you work and holds up a sign when something needs you. Sitting still is the whole job.

And yet my Mac was spending 12% of its processor on it, to sit still.

I did not need a long spec to fix that. I told Claude Code: "No theatrics." If it's idle, it's idle.

Now it uses almost none.

I keep this one as a habit. Software should cost nothing when it has nothing to do. That goes for a cat on a desktop, and for the tools a finance team runs all day.

A cat that sleeps should be cheap. Now it is.
